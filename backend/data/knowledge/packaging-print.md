## Process families

- **Offset litho**, for folding cartons. Excellent detail, high plate setup cost,
  economical at volume.
- **Flexography**, for corrugated. Coarser detail, cheaper plates, suited to
  large simple graphics.
- **Digital**, for short runs and versioning. No plates, higher cost per unit, and
  the only sane route below roughly 500 units.

## What drives the price

1. **The cutting die.** A one-off tooling cost per box shape. Reusing a partner
   standard size instead of a bespoke shape is the single biggest saving
   available on a small run.
2. **Colour count and plates**, for litho and flexo.
3. **Board grade and finish.** Lamination, spot UV and foil each add a pass.

## Practical notes

- Ask for a **flat sample and a made-up sample**. A design that looks right flat
  can put a fold or a glue flap straight through the logo.
- Kraft board shifts printed colour warm and darkens it. Colours proofed on white
  will not look the same on brown.
- Minimums are usually high, because the die and plate costs need spreading.
- Unprinted stock boxes plus a printed label often beat printed cartons outright
  below a few hundred units.
