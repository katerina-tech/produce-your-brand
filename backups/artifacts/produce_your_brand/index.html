01 — Process

How it works

Four steps. Nothing is sent to a real supplier until you approve it.

Describe
Plain language — product, quantity, deadline, finish. No form to fill in first.
Define
An editable production brief: product, ownership, customisation, potential method, location, deadline. You correct anything it got wrong.
Match
Compatibility scored against real supplier capabilities — method, materials, MOQ, customer-owned-goods acceptance, location, deadline. Unknowns are shown as unknown, never assumed compatible.
Request
A production-ready RFQ, generated and ready for you to review before it goes anywhere.
