## What the process does

Ink is picked up from an etched plate by a soft silicone pad, which then presses
it onto the product. Because the pad deforms, it reaches surfaces no flat process
can: curved, recessed, textured or stepped.

## Where it is the right answer

- Pens, tool handles, bottle caps, lids, small electronics housings.
- Compound curves and recesses.
- Small marks, roughly 60 mm and below, where the detail is fine.

## Constraints

- Each colour is a separate pass with its own plate, so cost and registration
  risk climb quickly beyond two or three colours.
- Print area is genuinely small. It is not an option for a large logo.
- Requires tooling per design, so it behaves like screen printing on cost: high
  setup, low per unit.
- Ink adhesion on untreated plastics often needs pre-treatment or a primer.
